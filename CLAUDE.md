# Claude review instructions

Follow `AGENTS.md` as the source of truth. During pull-request review:

- Review only the current diff and its consequences.
- Report concrete defects with evidence and tight line references.
- Prioritize privacy, authorization, measurement provenance, scaling and
  coordinate transforms, layout clearances, product identity, retries, and
  mobile resource use.
- Do not duplicate formatter, lint, or compiler output.
- Do not approve or modify the pull request.
